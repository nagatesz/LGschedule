LGschedulefor Flex

Your Flex schedule, always on your lock screen.

LGschedule turns the new Flex system into a live widget and a private controller: what class is now, what's next, and how long's left. Free for students.

How it works

Flex → LGschedule → your lock screen. Your widget calls your private link every minute; we fetch from Flex (cached 30s) and hand back clean JSON.

Privacy, plainly

No passwordsGoogle sign-in onlyToken encrypted (AES-256)Delete anytime

We never see or store a password. To fetch your schedule we do keep three things: your name and school email, your Flex session token (encrypted), and when it expires. Your schedule itself is never saved: it is fetched live and held in memory for 30 seconds. We don't read grades, messages, or anything else. Debug logs (with your name, never your token) are kept for about 24 hours. Only you can open your controller and script, and one click in your dashboard deletes everything we hold.
